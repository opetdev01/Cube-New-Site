## Founders & Leadership
Professor Dr. Ashraf Abdel Mohsen is the Founder and CEO of CUBE Consultants. He founded CUBE in 1990. Driven by his passion, he carefully selected a team of skilled designers and creatives to collaborate closely on projects, bringing unique visions to fruition. Abdel Mohsen’s goal was to promote sustainable urban expansion that adhered to international standards. He proposed a visionary plan named Cairo 2050 to enhance the urban quality of Cairo.

## The Cube Equation (Philosophy)
CUBE's design philosophy is built on three core pillars:
1. SPIRIT CARE: Design that nurtures the human soul by prioritizing emotional experience, peace, balance, and meaning. It creates spaces that heal, inspire, and connect.
2. EARTH CARE: Environmental responsibility as a core principle. True sustainability that coexists with nature rather than consuming it.
3. SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY: Integrating AI and smart systems to enhance performance, efficiency, and user experience to drive continuous innovation.

## Spirit Care 2026 Philosophy Elements
1. SOUL: Every space must feel alive through light, shadow, and proportion.
2. SUN: Light is the primary generator of life, dictating solar orientation and shading.
3. AIR: Creating movement and comfort through natural cross-ventilation.
4. WATER: A natural cooling agent that offers visual and spiritual reflection.
5. EARTH: Sustainability rooted deeply in context and local, natural materials.

## Services & Divisions
CUBE Consultants operates through 7 core divisions:
1. Architecture & Urban Design: Identity-driven, buildable design including Concept Design, Schematic & Detailed Design, Urban Planning & Masterplanning, and Interior Design.
2. Engineering & Consultancy: Precision and efficiency focusing on Multidisciplinary Coordination, Technical Tender Documents, Value Engineering, and Design Optimization.
3. Project Management: On-time delivery focusing on Planning & Scheduling, Budget Control, Site Supervision, and Quality Assurance.
4. City & Country Visions: Transforming cities through National Visions, City Identity, and Mega Concepts.
5. Development Advisory: Smarter investments focusing on Highest & Best Use, Feasibility Studies, and Market Analysis.
6. Sustainability (Environment & Sustainability): Future-ready projects focusing on Passive Design, Energy Systems, and Water Solutions.
7. Interior Design: Human-centered spaces focusing on Space Planning, Material Selection, and Lighting Design.
