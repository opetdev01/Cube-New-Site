Prof Dr. Ashraf Abdel Mohsen has been selected by the Egyptian Space Agency as Space Architecture Ambassador. This program has been founded with the aim of raising awareness of the importance and benefits of space exploration and research in Egypt. One of the key objectives is to inspire and encourage students in Egypt to pursue careers in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics), and to provide information on the fantastic opportunities this field offers. This program will help the Egyptian Space Agency reach a wider audience, and bring hope for the future of spaceflight in Egypt.
