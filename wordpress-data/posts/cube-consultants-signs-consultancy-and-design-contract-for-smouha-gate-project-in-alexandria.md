## In Collaboration with Marsoum Developments to Create an Integrated Mixed-Use Urban Destination

## Cairo – June 2026

## CUBE Consultants has announced the signing of a contract to provide engineering consultancy, urban planning, architectural design, and engineering design services for the Smouha Gate project in Alexandria on behalf of Marsoum Developments. This partnership reflects the confidence of leading real estate developers in CUBE’s expertise, built over more than three decades in the fields of urban planning and design.

## Located in one of Alexandria’s prime areas, the Smouha Gate project is envisioned as a comprehensive mixed-use destination featuring residential, commercial, administrative, medical, and entertainment components. The project aims to create a modern urban environment that seamlessly integrates living, working, services, and leisure within a cohesive urban framework.

## Under the agreement, CUBE Consultants will be responsible for preparing the project&#8217;s urban, architectural, and engineering studies and designs, with a strong focus on implementing the latest principles of sustainability, quality of life, and efficient resource utilization. These efforts will support the project’s vision while enhancing its investment and developmental value.

## Dr. Heba Soliman, Chief Executive Officer of CUBE Consultants, stated:

## &#8220;We are proud of the trust Marsoum Developments has placed in CUBE Consultants by selecting us as the consulting partner for the Smouha Gate project. We look forward to delivering an advanced urban model that reflects the latest global trends in planning and design while achieving a balance between user needs and the project&#8217;s investment objectives.&#8221;

## She added:

## &#8220;Through this project, we aim to create a fully integrated urban experience centered around people, providing a sustainable, high-quality environment that meets future aspirations and contributes to the ongoing urban development of Alexandria.&#8221;

## For his part, Eng. Ahmed El-Abbasy, Architectural Design Manager at CUBE Consultants, emphasized that the project represents both a unique design opportunity and an important professional challenge for the team.

## He stated:

## &#8220;The greatest challenge in the Smouha Gate project is achieving a balance between the human-centered requirements of the place and the project&#8217;s economic and investment considerations. The success of urban developments is not measured solely by the scale of investment or architectural components, but by their ability to create an integrated human environment that promotes comfort, quality of life, and long-term sustainability.&#8221;

## He added:

## &#8220;We seek to develop a design vision that transforms Smouha Gate into a vibrant urban destination characterized by high-quality public spaces, efficient mobility, and integrated activities, delivering maximum value to both users and investors alike.&#8221;

## This collaboration aligns with CUBE Consultants’ strategy of supporting high-impact urban development projects across Egypt and the region by delivering innovative planning and design solutions that address contemporary challenges and meet the evolving requirements of future cities.
