Cube Consultants Part of the Launch Event of “Nebu” by GEDICO Developments in the New Administrative Capital

Cube Consultants was honored to be a part in the official launch event of Nebu, a flagship development by GEDICO Developments, held in Egypt’s New Administrative Capital. The event, organized by GEDICO, brought together key figures from the real estate and design sectors to unveil this landmark project and celebrate the vision behind it.

The name “Nebu” is rooted in ancient Egyptian culture, meaning gold — a symbol of wealth, eternity, and divine power. Historically associated with the gods and the afterlife, gold represented purity and immortality. In today’s context, the name Nebu evokes a sense of luxury, heritage, and timelessness — qualities that are deeply embedded in the project’s architectural identity and brand philosophy.

Cube Consultants was engaged as a design partner on this prestigious project, collaborating with GEDICO Developments to craft a modern and integrated urban environment that reflects both cultural depth and contemporary needs. Our team contributed to the executive design of the project, ensuring that its architectural language aligns with the values of elegance, efficiency, and sustainability.

During the event, Cube Consultants’ Executive Design Manager delivered a speech highlighting the collaborative journey between the two companies. The speech emphasized the design vision behind Nebu, the challenges and opportunities of building within the New Capital, and the shared goal of setting new benchmarks in architectural excellence. It was a proud moment that reaffirmed our role not just as designers, but as long-term partners in shaping meaningful and iconic developments.

The launch event provided an engaging platform to connect with fellow industry leaders and celebrate a project that aspires to redefine mixed-use urban development in Egypt, Nebu is poised to become a cornerstone of the New Capital’s architectural and cultural landscape.

Cube Consultants looks forward to the continued progress of this visionary project and to supporting GEDICO Developments in bringing Nebu to life with the highest standards of design and execution.
