# Covid-19 Mobile Hospital

- **ID:** 5622
- **Date:** 2020-03-10T20:45:48
- **Slug:** covid19-mobile-isolation-hospital
- **Original Link:** https://cubeconsultants.org/covid19-mobile-isolation-hospital/

---

Due to COVID-19 (Coronavirus disease ) which began to spread globally by the beginning of 2020 on the first of March, CUBE Design team (CDS) immediately proposed the 72 hours Mobile hospitals that can be used in any location needed. The Cluster consist of 4 modular containers that can occupy 120 patients, the number of clusters used may vary according to different situation and locations. The Complex is fully functional with all services and infrastructure needed.
