TUTERA is a creative community hub for designers from all over the world to present designs reviving the Ancient Egyptian beauty and spirit in a way that fits today’s lifestyle and be relevant in today’s context. TUTERA seeks to bring true luxury back to Egypt as drawing international attention to Egypt, sensing a gap in the market for true lavish products. TUTERA captures the imagination with ultimate timeless and inspiring masterpiece designs that both us and customers find true pride in.

TUTERA aims to bring us back to the future with a new found perspective in the means of design taste and cultural pride. Egypt was once of the wealthiest kingdoms of the world filled with glamour and glory in every way. Egyptians need to retrieve their ethnic pride in a beautiful artistic fashion. Ancient Egyptian style is globally acknowledged and valued tremendously.

Founded by Cube Consultants, TUTERA began to subtly shine within the architectural and interior designs of their work incorporated in the slightest yet most effective details. But it does not stop here, TUTERA is meant for any type of design in architecture, interior, furniture, jewelry, fashion, apparel, automotive, film, music or any means of expression in art. This is our way of redefining and reviving our Ancient Egyptian heritage and pride. This is bringing our Ancient Egyptian taste and spirit back into the future.

We aim to revive the glory of Ancient Egypt.
We are based in Cairo, founded by Cube Consultants.
