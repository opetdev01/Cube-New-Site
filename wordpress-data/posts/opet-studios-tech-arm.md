## Introducing Opet Studios: The Digital Innovation Arm of Cube Consultants

Cube Consultants is proud to introduce Opet Studios, its dedicated technology and digital innovation arm, established to redefine how architecture, urban development, and real estate projects are experienced in the digital age.

As the industry evolves beyond traditional design and presentation methods, Opet Studios bridges the gap between vision and experience. It focuses on creating immersive, interactive, and intelligent digital solutions that enhance how projects are communicated, understood, and delivered.

Opet Studios specializes in a wide range of advanced digital services, including interactive masterplans, real-time web applications, augmented reality (AR) experiences, and smart visualization tools. These solutions enable clients, investors, and stakeholders to engage with projects in a dynamic and intuitive way—transforming static concepts into living, interactive experiences.

By integrating technology with design, Opet Studios empowers Cube Consultants to deliver not only architectural excellence but also cutting-edge digital innovation. This synergy strengthens Cube’s position as a forward-thinking consultancy capable of shaping both physical environments and digital experiences.

The launch of Opet Studios marks a strategic step toward the future—where architecture meets technology, and ideas are brought to life through interaction, intelligence, and immersive storytelling.
